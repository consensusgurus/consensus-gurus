What changed, and why

No tabs inside tabs
The page carried four tabs, and one of those held five more, so eight of the nine views were behind two clicks. It is one page now, seven sections in flow, with a jump bar. The pattern's rule is that the page is the thing.
The cards are gone
A white card on a near-black ground is a second ground nobody asked for. Every section sits on the page ground and is separated by a hairline, the way a converted board is.
Standing is one graphic in three layers
Yours (level, tier, IQ), the field (where you sit among the four at the top), and the handover (the chase, and the one filled control on the page). The old page split those across a stat bar, a card, a podium and a progress rail.
Categories take the ramp
Nine blocks, one per category, block width honest to how many dailies it holds. Same graphic as the home's "The day", counting your archive instead of today. The colours are lib/category-ramp.js exactly, both registers.
Figures, never prose
"Each game is worth 15, by where you finish, 15 for 1st, 12 for 2nd…" was four lines of small print. It is the ladder drawn, with your rung lit.
One accent
Sky, the step the Gauntlet run's CTA already takes, because the Stat Hub spans every category and cannot claim one. The nine steps are the only other colour on the page, and they only ever mean "this category".
The community board is a section, not a chip
Being #1 of 569 was a figure in a stat bar with nowhere to go, and /quizzes/leaderboard redirects back here. It is a real board now, with your share link beside it, since the board and the one action it asks for belong on the same screen.
Both registers
The switch in the cap is the stage's own. Nothing here is a new token: the palette is .stage-page and its light twin from globals.css.
