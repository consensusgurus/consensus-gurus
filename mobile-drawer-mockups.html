Mind Loft · the phone puzzle drawer, three directions

Same four moves as the slate above it: full bleed, no card borders, dark section bands, one figure strip per idea. All three drop the drawer's 13px panel padding and its five rounded bordered cards, so nothing floats in a white gutter, and each reuses an object the page already ships (the band from IN PROGRESS, the divided stat strip from the page header, the gold-ruled #1 row from the rails).

Today: the drawer is a 13px-padded white panel holding five rounded, bordered cards, with the 2×2 stat tiles bordered again inside one of them. Three levels of nesting and a white gutter down both sides, immediately under rows that run edge to edge.

A · Banded sections

The straight translation. Every card becomes a dark band plus edge-to-edge content: the 2×2 stat tiles become one 4-up divided strip (the page header's own object), the two leaderboards become full-width rows with the gold #1 and blue you treatment from the rails, the calendar spans the full width so the cells get bigger, and the drawer closes with a full-width bar instead of a floating button.
