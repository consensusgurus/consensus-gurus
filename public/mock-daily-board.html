Daily board: welding the top bar to the tiles

Three ways to close the light gap under the Up Next bar and give the tiles more variety. All three use the live tokens (navy #0e1d40, gold #e8b43a, Manrope) and the real game art, so what you see is what would ship.

Concept 1One slab

The hero, the filters, and the grid all move inside a single navy container, so there is no page ground showing anywhere between them. Tiles pop by sitting on a lighter raised surface than the container, each with a 4px accent cap and a soft wash of its own color behind the art.

Concept 2Welded header, state-coded tiles

The bar keeps its own identity but is physically joined to the board: rounded top corners only, and the filters become tabs that bridge the two. Uniformity is broken by state rather than decoration, so the board reads at a glance: finished tiles go green with a check, in-progress gets a gold ring, streak-at-risk gets a gold edge, and every icon sits in a tinted square of its own color.

Concept 3Up Next becomes a tile

The separate bar disappears entirely. Up Next becomes a large 2x2 feature tile inside the grid itself, so the hero and the board are literally the same object. Size does the work that color was doing: one big tile, small tiles around it, and the games whose streak is at risk sit on a lighter surface so they stand out from the finished ones.
