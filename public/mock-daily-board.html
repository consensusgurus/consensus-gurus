Daily board: a real stats bar, welded to reworked tiles

Nothing sits between the bar and the tiles in any of these: the filters moved into the bar as a segmented control, so the grid starts on the very next pixel. The bar is a bar, wide and dense, carrying six live stats instead of one progress line. Each concept then reworks the tiles a different way.

Concept 1Color cards

Every tile wears its own game color as the card itself, not a 3px stripe, so thirty games read as thirty things instead of one navy field. Finished games drop out of the color system entirely into a flat green state with the art dimmed, so what is left to play is obvious from across the room.

Concept 2Mosaic by priority

The tiles stop being equals. Games with a streak on the line become wide cards carrying the tag line and the streak, everything unplayed stays a normal tile, and the games you already finished collapse into a dim strip at the bottom. The board physically reorganizes around what you still have to do.

Concept 3Category bands

The six-across grid breaks into labeled bands, one per category, each with a colored rail and a done count on the left. The rhythm comes from the structure rather than from decorating each tile, and the bar welds straight onto the first band. Denser too, nine across instead of six.
