Daily tile: name first, icon under, with streak, leader and category

Same thirty games, same live data (today's real leaders, your real streaks and scores). The name leads at the top, the art drops underneath at a smaller size, and each tile now carries the category plus both your streak and the day's leader. Three ways to arrange the lower half.

Variant 1One meta line

Name, category eyebrow, icon, then a single line holding whichever signals apply: your score if you finished, your streak, and the day's leader. Tightest of the three and closest to the current tile height.

Variant 2Stacked meta

Same order, but your own status and the day's leader get their own lines. Easier to scan when a tile carries both, at the cost of about 12px of tile height.

Variant 3Category as a colored chip

The category becomes a small tinted chip in the game's own color rather than a grey eyebrow, which gives the grid a second layer of grouping you can read at a glance. Icon is smallest here to pay for it.
