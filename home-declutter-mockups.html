How the rail is sized. height: calc(100vh - 118px - 16px) where 118 is the sticky header (50) plus stat bar (56) plus a 12px gap, matching the --dh-fit measured-height pattern already used for the slate board. The rail is position: sticky, so the long centre column scrolls past it and the rail never leaves the screen.
Fixed cost inside it: panel head 42 + tabs 40 + four section bands at 33 = 214px. Everything left over goes to the one open section. At a 720px viewport that leaves 372px, which holds 10 leaderboard rows (330) or all 9 category rows (250) with room to spare. The open body carries overflow:auto purely as a safety net for anything shorter than 720.
Mobile is untouched. Every change here is scoped above 900px. The phone keeps the Direction B slate, the cap cards, the six-row peek and the drawer exactly as they ship today.

Mock A v3 · the rail now fits, by construction

  • The rail is pinned and measured, never taller than the viewport and never scrolling the page to reach it. Stacked sections became an accordion: all four bands stay visible, one body is open, and the open body takes exactly the leftover height.
  • A collapsed band still tells you the answer you came for. Each carries your standing on the right, "You #8", "You #3", "You lead 2", so nothing is lost by having three of them shut.
  • Today Combined now runs 10 deep rather than 8, because the accordion freed the room. Four boards in total: Today, By Category, This Week, All Time.
  • Use the 720 / 800 / 900 / 1080 selector in the bar above to check it at each height. The flag next to it measures the live rail and turns red if anything overflows.
  • Unchanged from v2: top cap two across with the Ready / Paused / Done pills and the date; Easiest and Familiar Favorite on the "Also today" line; streak in the stat bar; no duel or streak card in the rail.
  • Unchanged from v1: 63 rows folded into 9 category tiles that expand in place, search added, filter chips cut from 11 to 3.
  • Kept exactly: header, navy, blue ramp, gold, Manrope, panel head style, card style.
  • Above the fold: roughly 110 pieces down to 46, and the rail carries four leaderboards where it used to carry one.
